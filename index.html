Projects

WaypointAB

Students comparing Alberta post-secondary programs have to move between institution websites and separate labour-market tools. WaypointAB combines program details, admission requirements, related occupations, wage ranges, and job outlook into one searchable interface. The pilot covered two of Alberta's 26 publicly funded post-secondary institutions: MacEwan University and Bow Valley College. Labour-market figures were sourced from Statistics Canada and Job Bank data and seeded manually rather than pulled live. Province-wide coverage and live labour-market updates remained outside the scope of the term.
